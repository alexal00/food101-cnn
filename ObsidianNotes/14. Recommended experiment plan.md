|Model|small CNN|

## Experiment 1: custom CNN baseline
|Item|Value|
|---|---|
|Classes|10 first, then optionally 101|
|Model|custom CNN|
|Epochs|10–20|
|Goal|Establish pedagogical baseline|
Expected conclusion:
A CNN trained from scratch can learn local visual features, but it is data- and compute-hungry compared with transfer learning.

## Experiment 2: EfficientNet-B0 transferlearning
|Item|Value|
|---|---|
|Classes|101|
|Model|EfficientNet-B0|
|Training|frozen head training + partial fine-tuning|
|Goal|Main local-performance model|
Expected conclusion:
Pretrained convolutional features provide a stronger representation and faster convergence than a from-scratch CNN.
