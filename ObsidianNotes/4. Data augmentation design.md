Conservative augmentation is implemented:
```text
RandomResizedCrop or padded resize with mild scale jitter
RandomHorizontalFlip
Moderate ColorJitter
Small random rotation
No vertical flip
No random erasing
No CutMix
No MixUp
```
Initial augmentation recommended:
```python
train_transform:
    EXIF correction
    RGB conversion
    resize/pad to 224
    random horizontal flip p=0.5
    color jitter:
        brightness=0.15
        contrast=0.15
        saturation=0.10
        hue=0.03
    small rotation ±10°
    ImageNet normalization

eval_transform:
    EXIF correction
    RGB conversion
    resize/pad to 224
    ImageNet normalization
```

Conservative augmentation is encouraged since:

|Augmentation|Decision|Reason|
|---|--:|---|
|Horizontal flip|Use|Usually does not change food class identity.|
|Vertical flip|Avoid|Upside-down food is visually unrealistic.|
|Color jitter|Moderate|Helps with lighting variation but should not distort food color identity.|
|Random erasing|Avoid|May remove the most discriminative food region.|
|MixUp/CutMix|Avoid|Useful in some classifiers, but less pedagogically transparent and complicates interpretability.|
